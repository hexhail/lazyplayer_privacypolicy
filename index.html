Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

In short
  • You can use Lazy Player as a guest. Your characters then stay on your device, and we receive nothing from you.
  • If you create an account, we store your email and your characters so you can use them on any device.
  • We measure how the app is used, to improve it. You can turn that off in Settings.
  • We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and everything in it at any time.

Who we are

Lazy Player is a free character companion for tabletop role-playing games, available at lazy-player-b0134.web.app. It is an independent project run by the Lazy Player team, based in Argentina, which is responsible for the data described here. To contact us about your data, write to thelazyplayer@proton.me.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Email address and password When you sign up with email To create your account and let you log in. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store them.
Name, email and profile picture from Google When you sign in with Google To create your account. We only receive what Google shares when you agree to it.
Display name If you enter one when signing up To show it in your account.
Your characters When you are logged in To save them to your account and sync them across your devices. This includes everything you enter on a character: stats, spells, inventory, notes, and any character picture you upload.
Usage data While you use the app, unless you turn it off To understand which features are used and where the app fails, and improve it. See Analytics.

We don't ask for your real name, location, phone number or payment details.

Guest mode

If you continue as a guest, your characters are saved only in your browser on that device. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes them. If you later create an account, the characters on that device are uploaded to it.

Analytics

We use PostHog, hosted in the European Union, to measure how the app is used: which screens are opened, which features are used (for example creating a character, leveling up or exporting a PDF), and errors. If you are logged in, this is linked to a random account identifier, never to your email.

Text you type (such as passwords and notes) is never recorded. You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings.

Who processes your data

We use these services to run the app. They process data on our behalf and only for these purposes:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database and Hosting), operated by Google LLC: accounts, saved characters and serving the app. Firebase privacy information.
  • PostHog, hosted in the EU: usage analytics.

These providers may store data in countries other than yours, including the United States and the European Union, under their own security and data-protection commitments.

We don't sell your personal data, share it with advertisers, or use it to show ads.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and characters: until you delete them or ask us to delete your account.
  • Analytics data: up to 1 year.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to send you a copy of it, by writing to thelazyplayer@proton.me from the email on your account. We will answer within 10 days. Deleting your account deletes your characters with it, and this can't be undone.

If you are in Argentina, you may also contact the Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública, the authority that enforces Law 25.326 on personal data protection. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you have the rights given by the GDPR, including filing a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

Lazy Player is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each account can only read and change its own characters. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you if a breach affects it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we will let you know in the app.

Lazy Player is unofficial fan content. It is not approved or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.
